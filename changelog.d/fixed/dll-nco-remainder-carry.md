- **A `Dll` command under one NCO step is no longer dropped.** The 32-bit `phase_inc` conversion truncates, so a command smaller than one step (2^-32 cycles per sample) vanished every update, which left long codes and large samples per chip with a dead zone: the one-segment loop at 1023 chips and narrow `bn` read 3.6 to 13.9 times its formula, and the four-segment loop 5 to 44 times. The truncated remainder is now carried into the next update, so the mean increment follows the command; one-segment rows at 1023 chips read 1.13 to 1.29 times the formula. The state blob gains the remainder (version 17). A stream that ends exactly on a period boundary leaves the replica within one step of the wrap either side, so a count there is not deterministic; examples end mid-period ([#3249](https://github.com/doppler-dsp/doppler/issues/3249)).
