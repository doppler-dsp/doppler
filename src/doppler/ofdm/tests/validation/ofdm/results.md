## 2. Characterisation

### 2.1 The float32 error of a render, per transform length (`ofdm_fft_error.c`; C section 11)

One class of `n_fft` points, every subcarrier a seeded unit-power QPSK point, scale `1/sqrt(n_fft)`, no prefix and no window, so a body is exactly the header's sum. The error is the relative l2 distance of one body from that sum in double (written out up to 1024 points, the library's cf64 transform above), the worst over the symbols. The bound is derived for a power of two from the transform that runs (section 11 of the C test): `L eta / (1 - L eta) + 2u`, `eta = mu + gamma_4 (sqrt 2 + mu)`, `mu < 20u`, `L = log2 n_fft`, `u = 2^-24`. The twiddles are formed in float (pffft.c:1141, 1157, 1161, 1264), so `mu` is 20u and not u: the inventory's first figure, 2.4e-6 at N = 64, assumed twiddles rounded from double and is replaced by 9.3e-6 here (F3).

## 3. Review

- **F1 · FIXED** — Reading `ofdm_core.h` against `test_ofdm_core.c` found 41 claims: 28 pinned, 5 pinned only at literals (a gain on a fixed entry, three of five builders re-opening a sealed layout, reset of an unsealed layout, the accepted edges `cp == useful` and `window == symbol`) and 8 absent (the `OFDM_N_FFT_MAX`, `OFDM_N_PHASE_MAX` and `OFDM_COUNT_MAX` edges, `state_bytes` over a render, one plan per length, `modulate_max_out` past the end and for no points, `finish` before a render, a fixed entry on a null subcarrier). Section 11 pins each beside its control, and each went red under its own sabotage. One half of one claim has no sabotage: `modulate_max_out (0)` is 0 when the next symbol needs points because `walk()` returns 0 there by construction, and no mutant short of an absurd one reaches it.

- **F2 · FIXED** — `dp_ofdm_add_class` refused a NaN `scale`, which its header did not say. Under the library's rule a NaN parameter is garbage in (#2103), and the scale reaches only the samples and the layout hash: no size, index or abort. Nothing refuses it, and no test pins a refusal.

- **F3 · FIXED** — The claim inventory first gave the 64-point bound as 2.4e-6, taking the twiddles as accurate to u. pffft forms each twiddle's angle in float with three roundings, so the error is under 20u and the bound 9.3e-6. The test derives it from those lines, and says the condition under which it stops applying: the plan leaving pffft (pocketfft.c:71's gate) or the twiddle formation changing.

- **F4 · BY DESIGN** — The error depends on which transform the length takes, not on its size alone. A multiple of 16 that is 5-smooth runs pffft in float: about 1e-7 to 2.3e-7, rising as `sqrt (log2 N)` and at least 39 times under the derived bound at every power of two. Any other length runs pocketfft in double and rounds once: 448, 704 and 832 read about 4e-8, under u. The pffft lengths that are not powers of two (192, 320, 576, 960) read 1.4e-7 to 3.1e-7, and no bound is derived for their radix-3 and radix-5 passes. The header makes no accuracy claim and the design page asked for the measurement; this is it, and nothing is asserted where nothing is derived.
