## 2. Characterisation

### 2.1 The error a window adds to a symbol body (C 10e, 10f)

Each SIGNAL and Data symbol's 64 M-sample transform, starting `start` samples into the symbol, against the same transform of the unwindowed render at the same timing, on the 52 occupied subcarriers; 54 Mb/s, M = 4, w and start in units of M. The window weights only a symbol's first w samples (its own rise, the previous symbol's fall), so for w <= start the body is the same samples and the error is exactly zero. Above it the expectation, for independent unit-power symbols, is `EVM^2 = 2 sum_{j=start}^{w-1} fall_j^2 / (64 M)`, `fall_j = cos^2 (pi (j + 1/2) / (2 w))`, and the measured occupied-subcarrier error can never exceed the time-domain error ratio (Parseval: the unwindowed body has all its energy on the occupied subcarriers). Start 16 is a receiver taking the last 64 M samples; start 8 is one sampling eight 20 MHz samples early. The rows are `data/evm.csv`.

### 2.2 The transmit spectral mask (17.3.9.3)

A Welch average of periodic-Hann frames of 512 M samples, hop 256 M, zero-padded 8 times, taken only inside one PPDU's SIGNAL and Data samples (a frame across two PPDUs would carry an uncrossfaded cut) or, in the `ppdu` rows, over the whole PPDU. The 100 kHz resolution bandwidth is realized by integrating the padded bins within 50 kHz of the frequency (97.7 kHz realized: a power-of-two transform cannot land on 100 kHz at fs = 20 M MHz), and a level is dBr against the largest such in-band sum, the larger of the two sides. The standard error of a level, for K frames of 50% overlapped Hann (overlap correlation 1/6), not crediting the integration: `se = (10 / ln 10) sqrt ((1 + 2/36) / K)`. 54 Mb/s, 4095-octet PSDUs. The full sweep's rows are `data/mask.csv`.

Predicted on paper before the run, from the expected PSD of the discrete symbol pulse, unwindowed, against the in-band peak: -24.9 / -32.4 / -35.2 dBr at M = 4 and -25.0 / -33.0 / -36.7 at M = 8, failing -40 at 30 MHz; with the standard's 2 M transition -27.5 / -55.0 / -68.9, passing.

## 3. Review

- **F1 · FIXED** — Reading `wlan_core.h` against `test_wlan_core.c` found 38 claims: 31 pinned (the eight Table 17-6 pairs and the refusal of the other eight, oversampling 1 to 128, `ppdu_len`, the scrambler, every `_why` sentence), 2 at literals and 5 absent. Added: the window reaches a symbol body only above 16 M (32 against 33 at M = 2, bit for bit), each argument refusal of `dp_wlan_layout` leaving the modulator untouched (six rows), and the QAM unit-power tolerance derived as 4 sigma of the rate's own symbol count instead of a fixed 0.03 that passed at 2.25 sigma on one seed.

- **F2 · FIXED** — `dp_wlan_layout` checked its window only through the modulator: for 80 M < window <= 160 M the L-STF and L-LTF classes were added to the caller's modulator before the SIGNAL class refused. The window ceiling is one helper that `dp_wlan_params_why` and the layout's up-front guard both call, and the header says a refusal by the arguments leaves the modulator untouched (C 10f).

- **F3 · FIXED** — `dp_wlan_create` re-checked the rate after `dp_wlan_params_why` had passed. Every in-range pair builds (C section 1) and the allocation aborts rather than return NULL, so the branch was dead, and it was the only way `dp_wlan_create_why` could have returned NULL with its reason unset. `dp_wlan_create` has no such branch.

- **F4 · FIXED** — `Wlan` rendered with no window by default, and an oversampled PPDU then failed the transmit mask's 30 MHz point by 4.6 dB at M = 4 and 3.1 dB at M = 8 (§2.2). The default is now the standard's 2 M transition, which passes every point with 7.9 dB to spare; `window=0`, by asking, still fails by those margins, as a stated property of the rectangular choice. At M = 1 the mask beyond 10 MHz is not representable at all. wfmgen's ofdm source takes the same default: its samples equal `Wlan`'s at M = 1, 2 and 4 (`test_the_default_window_is_the_standards_100_ns` in `src/doppler/wfm/tests/test_ofdm_source.py`, and the C pin `test_the_default_window_is_the_standards_100_ns`), so the mask result carries over to it (#3190).

- **F5 · BY DESIGN** — The estimator that ran (20 integrated padded Hann bins) has an expected in-band peak-to-mean of 1.25 dB, derived by convolving the symbol pulse's PSD with the Hann kernel's power response; it reads 1.58 dB at K = 368 and 1.97 dB at K = 92. The excess is the upward bias of a maximum taken over a noisy estimate, and it scales as the noise does: 0.72 dB at K = 92 and 0.33 dB at K = 368, a ratio of 2.2 against sqrt (4) = 2. The reference reads high, so every dBr level is conservative by about that much.

- **F6 · BY DESIGN** — A window is free for a receiver that takes the last 64 M samples as long as it stays inside the 16 M guard interval, and costs from the first sample past it; a receiver sampling 8 M early pays from w > 8 M. The header's sentence (above 16 M the cross-fade reaches the body) is the first case; §2.1 gives both, so a caller choosing a window above the standard's 2 M can read its cost here.

- **F7 · GAP** — Annex I's stage-by-stage example (the SIGNAL and Data bits, their coded and interleaved forms, the subcarrier values and the time samples), the FCS byte order over its PSDU, and the pilot polarity against Eq. 17-25's printed values are not held: the text is not transcribed in the tree. The PPDU is held against an in-build oracle and the tables against their printed forms instead (C sections 9, 10c). #3148.
