- **The specan NATS sources read through one ring, linear in the backlog.** `SocketSource` and `PullSource` share an `F32Buffer` ring and a single read path; a drain of `T` samples no longer copies the whole backlog on every packet. Drain time at `2T` over `T` is about 2.3 to 2.4 on the measured host, where it was 2.6 to 8.1 (#1894). The contract, including when a `PullSource` frame is acked, is in `docs/design/specan.md`.
