- **Acquisition delivers ~1.65× the false-alarm rate a caller configures, and
    it is now measured, decomposed and ratcheted.** The threshold sizes
    `pfa_cell` from the native cell count while the gate is a maximum over the
    Doppler-*interpolated* surface. Two errors of opposite sign:
    `N_eff(1)/N` = 0.89 (the native model is conservative) × `N_eff(2)/N_eff(1)`
    = 1.86 (what interpolation adds). Only `N` is wrong — the ratio holds
    across three decades of target, which clears the per-cell threshold and
    the CFAR conversion. Certification records it as F7 and ratchets it;
    `docs/design/dsss-acquisition.md` §9.1 derives the model.
