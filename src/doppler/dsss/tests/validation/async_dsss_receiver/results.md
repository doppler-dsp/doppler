## 3. Review — findings, with verdicts

- **F1 · GAP** — `steps_max_out()` returns 0, which is not a bound: a call that completes a carried code period emits that whole period's symbols, so at more than one symbol per period an `out` sized to the call's `x_len` drops symbols silently. At SPEC (0.9 symbols a period) it never binds. Tracked as #2469.

- **F2 · GAP** — `differential` is inert here: it reaches MpskReceiver's `bits()` demap, and this object returns soft symbols only (`_test_differential_does_not_change_the_symbols`). Remove it or add `bits()`: #2471.

- **F3 · CONFIRMED** — A first hand-off can land off the carrier and never pull in, at the 10 dB design point as well as below it. With `reacquire` off (the binding's default, and this harness's), the full sweep has 3 of its 10 quality draws under 10 dB with no symbol lock within 4 s, 5 of 20 arrivals at 5 dB unlocked at 2 s, and dropout draws that never settled in 8 of 28 at 5 dB and 1 of 21 at 10 dB (section 2.5). The 10 dB draw is on #2409: the refine handed over outside the carrier loops' pull-in, code lock came up and symbol lock never did. `reacquire` recovers a missed hand-off by searching again; the SPEC example locks every draw that way. The hand-off accuracy is #2409.

- **F4 · C-ONLY** — The lock detector's hysteresis, the release clock's edges and the hand-over domain are certified symbol by symbol and sample by sample in the C tests of section 1; the binding exposes only the flags, which section 2 reads.

- **F5 · GAP** — The error rate sits 0.89 to 1.00 dB from the BPSK bound over the sweep (section 2.6), and nothing yet says where that goes. The EVM over the same windows is only +0.22 to +0.48 dB over its ideal, so most of the loss is not in the error's power. The error is not circular: the blind M2M4, which assumes it is, reads 2.41 to 2.83 dB above the applied Es/N0, against `dp_ber_report()`'s 3 dB cross-check. A diagnostic on #2495 finds the in-phase error 3 dB under the quadrature one and heavy-tailed, and adjacent symbols' in-phase errors anti-correlated. Accounting for the loss by stage is #2495.

## 4. Limits — claims a caller may rely on

The EVM bound is two-sided and set per Es/N0, as a loss over the meter's ideal. A locked receiver's error is the ideal's plus an implementation floor, EVM² = ideal + floor in linear power, so the most loss it may show is 10·log10(1 + floor/ideal) plus 0.30 dB, the EVM estimator's own counting noise over 2700 symbols (3.6 sigma of 0.08 dB): +1.65 dB at 20 dB, +0.78 dB at 15 dB, +0.47 dB at 10 dB. The floor, -24.4 dB, is what the full sweep's worst 20 dB draw leaves. An EVM more than 0.30 dB under the ideal fails too: that is a meter or harness defect, not a better receiver. At 15 dB the model reads high (the sweep's worst loss there is +0.13 dB), so that bound is the looser one. Below 10 dB the loss comes from the loops' dynamics rather than a floor, and no EVM limit is claimed.
