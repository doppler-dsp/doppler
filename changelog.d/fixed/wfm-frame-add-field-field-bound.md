- **A frame's sizes are checked where they are laid out (#3321).** A field or derived width near 2^63 bits wrapped the frame's size, and assembly wrote past its buffer, through the C API and through a scene's `"bits"`. The layout now refuses a wrapped description, and a scene's `"bits"` must be a whole number below 2^53. Frames of any length that fits a `size_t` are unchanged.
