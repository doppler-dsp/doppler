- **`doppler.dsss.handoff.dll_init_chip_from_acq` is removed; use the hit's `chip_phase`.** Every `Acquisition` hit now carries `chip_phase`, the Dll seed in chips, computed once in C with the dwell advance applied when a carrier is set (the Python copy did not carry it: 0.9 chip late at 20 ppm on the 40 dB-Hz floor). Replace `dll_init_chip_from_acq(hit.code_phase, spc, sf)` with `hit.chip_phase`. See [#1257](https://github.com/doppler-dsp/doppler/issues/1257).
