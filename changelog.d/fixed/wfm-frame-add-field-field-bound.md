- **`dp_wfm_frame_add_field` refuses a field past the Field bound (#3321).** A `LEN`, a `REPS` or their product above 261,120 bits is now refused at the call, as the Field text already was. Before, a length near 2^63 wrapped the frame's size and assembly wrote out of bounds on the heap; `dp_wfm_frame_add_derived` refuses the same width. A caller passing a literal longer than 261,120 bits now gets -1 where it was accepted.
